UK Music Festival
Weather Analysis

Scraped data for 206 UK festivals and combined it with 30 years of historical weather data
from Open-Meteo, scoring and comparing festivals by their typical weather.

Summary

Which UK festivals have the best weather? I scraped details of 206 UK festivals for 2025, then cleaned and geocoded them. For each festival, I pulled weather for the same dates in every year from 1995 to 2024 using the Open-Meteo API. I combined temperature, rain days (over 5mm) and wind into a Festival Weather Score using z-scores, and built Tableau dashboards to compare festivals by region, genre and month.

Key Technologies

Screaming Frog, Google Sheets, Python (pandas, NumPy, SciPy), Open-Meteo API, RStudio, Tableau

Key Findings

  • Festivals — typical duration is 3 days, July hosts the most festivals but August has the highest capacity, and South East England holds the most festivals.
  • Weather — the driest festival is Cross the Tracks (3.3% chance of significant rainfall), the warmest is Jazz Cafe Festival (14.3–22.4°C), and the best overall weather is at Tolpuddle Martyrs Festival and Rally.

Skills Demonstrated

Web scraping, data cleaning and transformation, API integration, statistical analysis, geographic data processing, data visualisation in Tableau
